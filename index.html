Who should make the creative?

Creator or brand creative is usually decided by belief. I ran the head-to-head instead: two equal-spend, three-day reach-and-frequency flights in the same month, $580K each, with identical targeting and placements. Only the creative changed.

Give every placement a job

Premium placements usually get compared on one CPM line, and the cheapest one is kept. I ran $1.36M across TopView, TopFeed and Pulse and read each placement on its own metric.

Measure lift, not likes

An entertainment launch judged on tune-in, not views. One campaign reached 15% of the U.S. population and was read on lift. A three-way creative test on tune-in put brand creative ahead of creator-boosted posts; AI-generated creative finished last.
