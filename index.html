About

I plan, run and measure paid social for national brands, and I build the tools that make the work hold up.

Four years at two agencies, promoted twice in two years, most recently running a $20.5M annual plan across six channels in two countries. The work ran from matched-spend creative tests and brand lift studies to premium TikTok placements and a Pinterest launch — and, when a four-day, four-person review of 2,000 ads was the bottleneck, to Buildcheck: an AI-assisted QA system I designed, specified and rolled out across the agency's Meta and TikTok teams.

What I want next is an in-house seat, where organic and paid sit together, the comments feed the brief, and one calendar holds both. The point of view says what I'd change and why; the demo shows how I make the build boring.
